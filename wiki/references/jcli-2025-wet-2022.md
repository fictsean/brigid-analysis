# Journal of Climate 2025 — A Multiscale Evaluation of the Wet 2022 in Eastern Australia

**Citation**: "A Multiscale Evaluation of the Wet 2022 in Eastern Australia." *Journal of Climate*,
38(4) (2025). (See DOI for the full author list.)

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1175/JCLI-D-24-0224.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JCLI-D-24-0224.1)

## Summary

A multiscale analysis of the exceptionally wet 2022 across eastern Australia, attributing the
anomalies primarily to La Niña and warm sea-surface temperatures.

## Why it matters here

Like [[comm-earth-env-2025-feb2022-floods]], it characterises the 2022 floods but reports **no
probabilistic attribution**, leaving our PR=1.11 the sole quantitative estimate.
