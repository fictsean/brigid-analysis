# Matthews et al. 2009 — Proportionality of warming to cumulative carbon emissions

**Citation**: Matthews, H. D., Gillett, N. P., Stott, P. A., and Zickfeld, K. (2009). "The
proportionality of global warming to cumulative carbon emissions." *Nature*, 459(7248), 829–832.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08047](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08047)

## Summary

Establishes the near-linear relationship between cumulative CO₂ emissions and global warming (the
TCRE / carbon-climate response), largely independent of emission pathway.

## Why it matters here

TCRE linearity is the physical justification for our proportional apportionment: each entity's
warming share ≈ its cumulative-emissions share (the FaIR ΔT cancels in the ratio). See
[[emissions-to-forcing]].
