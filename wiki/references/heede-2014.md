# Heede 2014 — Tracing emissions to fossil fuel and cement producers

**Citation**: Heede, R. (2014). "Tracing anthropogenic carbon dioxide and methane emissions to fossil
fuel and cement producers, 1854–2010." *Climatic Change*, 122(1), 229–241.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1007/s10584-013-0986-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10584-013-0986-y)

## Summary

The foundational "Carbon Majors" study: traces 914 GtCO₂e — **63% of cumulative industrial CO₂ and
methane emissions, 1751–2010** — to 90 investor-owned, state-owned, and nation-state producers. The
methodological and data basis for the Carbon Majors database we use.

## Why it matters here

Source of the ~63–71% Carbon-Majors **emissions** coverage figure. Note: this is a share of
*emissions*, **not** a share of *warming* — our warming-share apportionment (~54%) is a distinct
quantity (see [[2026-06-24-literature-cross-check]]).
