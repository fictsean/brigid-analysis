# IPCC AR6 WG1 (2021) — Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis

**Citation**: IPCC (2021). *Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis. Contribution of Working
Group I to the Sixth Assessment Report of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change*. Cambridge
University Press.

**Link**: [https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/)

## Summary

The IPCC Sixth Assessment physical-science report. We use its assessed **human-induced warming of
~1.07 °C for 2010–2019** (vs 1850–1900) and the assessed ECS (2.5–4 °C) / TCR (1.2–2.4 °C) ranges.

## Why it matters here

The primary validation anchor for the FaIR ensemble: our median gives ~1.04–1.06 °C for 2011–2020,
matching the AR6 best estimate. Pinned in the validation harness.
