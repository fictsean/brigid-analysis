# Hersbach et al. 2020 — The ERA5 global reanalysis

**Citation**: Hersbach, H., Bell, B., Berrisford, P., Hirahara, S., Horányi, A., Muñoz-Sabater, J.,
et al. (2020). "The ERA5 global reanalysis." *Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society*,
146(730), 1999–2049.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.3803](https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.3803)

## Summary

The reference paper for ERA5, ECMWF's fifth-generation atmospheric reanalysis (~31 km, hourly, 1940–
present). The observational backbone for our event-attribution work.

## Why it matters here

ERA5 daily maximum temperature (mx2t) and total precipitation supply the factual climate "pool" for
the GEV shift-fits and the observed regional amplification factors. (The downloaded products are
accessed via the Copernicus CDS; the dataset DOI is recorded on [[era5-reanalysis]].)
