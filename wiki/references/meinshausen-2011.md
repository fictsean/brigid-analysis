# Meinshausen et al. 2011 — MAGICC6

**Citation**: Meinshausen, M., Raper, S. C. B., and Wigley, T. M. L. (2011). "Emulating coupled
atmosphere–ocean and carbon cycle models with a simpler model, MAGICC6 – Part 1: Model description
and calibration." *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 11, 1417–1456.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-11-1417-2011](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-11-1417-2011)

## Summary

Describes MAGICC6, the reduced-complexity climate emulator. The model used by [[ekwurzel-2017]] for
the original carbon-major warming attribution.

## Why it matters here

The methodological ancestor of our emissions→warming step; we use the more recent, AR6-constrained
FaIR ([[leach-2021]]) instead, but MAGICC6 sets the precedent.
