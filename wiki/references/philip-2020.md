# Philip et al. 2020 — A protocol for probabilistic extreme event attribution

**Citation**: Philip, S., Kew, S., van Oldenborgh, G. J., Otto, F., Vautard, R., van der Wiel, K.,
King, A., Lott, F., Arrighi, J., Singh, R., and van Aalst, M. (2020). "A protocol for probabilistic
extreme event attribution analyses." *Advances in Statistical Climatology, Meteorology and
Oceanography*, 6, 177–203.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.5194/ascmo-6-177-2020](https://doi.org/10.5194/ascmo-6-177-2020)

## Summary

The standard World Weather Attribution methodology paper — defines the covariate-based nonstationary
GEV/GPD shift-fit, the probability-ratio framework, and the validation steps used across WWA studies.

## Why it matters here

Our PR step ([[far-probability-ratio]]) implements this protocol: a nonstationary GEV shift-fit with
a smoothed-GMST covariate referenced to pre-industrial, additive for temperature and multiplicative
(Clausius-Clapeyron) for precipitation.
