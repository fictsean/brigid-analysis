# Stuart-Smith et al. 2025 — Carbon majors and the scientific case for climate liability

**Citation**: "Carbon majors and the scientific case for climate liability." *Nature* (2025).
(Companion perspective to the systematic heatwave-attribution study [[stuart-smith-2025]]; see DOI
for the full author list.)

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08751-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08751-3)

## Summary

Argues that end-to-end attribution from individual carbon majors' emissions to specific climate
impacts is now scientifically robust enough to inform climate-liability litigation — the conceptual
frame for exactly the chain this project builds.

## Why it matters here

Validates the legal-science premise of the project and accompanies the quantitative benchmark in
[[stuart-smith-2025]].
