# Comm. Earth & Environment 2025 — How February 2022 redefines extreme floods in Australia

**Citation**: "How February 2022 redefines extreme floods in Australia." *Communications Earth &
Environment* (2025). (See DOI for the full author list.)

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-025-02307-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-025-02307-z)

## Summary

Characterises the record February 2022 eastern-Australia flood event and its place in the
observational record, emphasising the La Niña + warm-SST drivers.

## Why it matters here

One of the few peer-reviewed treatments of the 2022 SE QLD floods. It gives **no probabilistic
attribution (PR)**, so our PR=1.11 remains the sole quantitative estimate, but it qualitatively
supports our conservative framing.
