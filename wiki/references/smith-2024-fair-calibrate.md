# Smith et al. 2024 — fair-calibrate v1.4.1

**Citation**: Smith, C. J., et al. (2024). "fair-calibrate v1.4.1: calibration, constraining, and
validation of the FaIR simple climate model for reliable future climate projections."
*Geoscientific Model Development*, 17, 8569–8592.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-17-8569-2024](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-17-8569-2024)

## Summary

The calibration/constraining workflow that produces the FaIR posterior parameter ensemble
(constrained against observed warming, ocean heat content, and CMIP6), covering the IPCC AR6 assessed
ECS/TCR ranges.

## Why it matters here

We use its 841-member constrained posterior as the FaIR climate-configuration ensemble — the source
of our 5–95th-percentile warming uncertainty. (Earlier wiki text mis-attributed fair-calibrate v1.4
to "Leach et al. 2024"; the calibration paper is Smith et al. 2024 — corrected 2026-06-24. The FaIR
model itself is [[leach-2021]].)
