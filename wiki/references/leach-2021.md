# Leach et al. 2021 — FaIRv2.0.0

**Citation**: Leach, N. J., Jenkins, S., Nicholls, Z., Smith, C. J., Lynch, J., Cain, M., Walsh, T.,
Wu, B., Tsutsui, J., and Allen, M. R. (2021). "FaIRv2.0.0: a generalized impulse response model for
climate uncertainty and future scenario exploration." *Geoscientific Model Development*, 14,
3007–3036.

**Link**: [https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-14-3007-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-14-3007-2021)

## Summary

Describes the FaIR v2 reduced-complexity climate model — the emulator we run for the emissions→warming step.

## Why it matters here

We run FaIR v2.2 with the fair-calibrate posterior ([[smith-2024-fair-calibrate]]) to produce the
global temperature ensemble and per-entity warming contributions.
